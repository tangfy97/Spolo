# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Spolo is a macOS app for merging photos and videos into one video, made by TangSpark. This policy covers every version of Spolo: the Mac App Store version and the one downloaded from tang-spark.no.

**Spolo collects no data.** It has no network access at all; the operating system's App Sandbox does not allow it to connect to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or update checks, and no third-party code.

What Spolo does on your Mac:

- It reads the photos and videos you add, to show them and merge them.
- To bring back your clips the next time you open it, it remembers which files you added and how you edited them, as bookmarks to those files. It does not copy them.
- Photos and videos you add from your Photos library are chosen in the system's photo picker, so Spolo never has access to the library itself. The items you pick are copied into Spolo's own folder, because they have no file of their own; once they are no longer among your clips, they are deleted the next time Spolo opens.
- It writes files only where you ask: the place you save the merged video.

None of this leaves your Mac. Links in the Help menu open in your web browser, which is outside Spolo; "Check for a New Version" in the downloaded version tells the Spolo web page which version you have, so it can say whether a newer one exists.
